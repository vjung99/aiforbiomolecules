AI Models of Biomolecules Focus Group

Welcome to the AI Models of Biomolecules Focus Group, a community of researchers and practitioners exploring the transformative application of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to biomolecular sciences.

Our focus group organizes regular activities such as seminars, networking events, and discussions on the latest methodologies and applications. We bring together experts to discuss cutting-edge AI models (such as AlphaFold, protein language models, and generative models) for structural biology, molecular dynamics, and drug design.
